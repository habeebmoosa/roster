# Refund policy

Roster ships a 30-day, no-questions-asked refund window on every plan. The clock starts on the
original purchase date, not the most recent renewal, unless the customer purchased a new plan
tier (in which case the window restarts from the upgrade date).

## Eligibility

- Any purchase within the last 30 days qualifies automatically.
- Annual plans refunded after day 30 but within 90 days are eligible for a prorated refund
  covering the unused months, minus a $15 processing fee.
- Refunds are not available for gifted or promotional (free) credits — only for real charges.
- A customer may request at most one refund per 12-month period on the same account.

## How to process a refund

1. Confirm the purchase date and amount in the billing dashboard.
2. Confirm the request falls inside the eligibility window above.
3. If eligible, issue the refund to the original payment method. Refunds post within 5-10
   business days depending on the customer's bank.
4. If the purchase is outside the standard window but within the 90-day prorated window,
   calculate the prorated amount, subtract the $15 fee, and note the calculation in the ticket.
5. If the customer disputes the $15 fee, it may be waived once per account at agent discretion
   for a first-time refund request.

## Common exceptions

- **Duplicate charges**: always fully refundable regardless of date, no fee.
- **Fraudulent charges**: escalate to the billing team immediately rather than refunding directly.
- **Chargebacks already filed with the bank**: do not issue a refund — the payment processor
  handles these separately. Inform the customer the chargeback is already in progress.
