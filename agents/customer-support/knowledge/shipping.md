# Shipping policy

Roster is a software product, but physical merchandise (branded swag from the referral program)
ships through a third-party fulfillment partner. This doc covers swag shipping only — software
access itself is never "shipped," it's provisioned instantly on purchase.

## Timelines

- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days within the US, 10-14 business days internationally.
- Expedited shipping (paid upgrade): 2-3 business days within the US only.
- Orders placed after 2pm ET ship the next business day.

## Tracking

- A tracking link is emailed automatically once the fulfillment partner marks the order shipped,
  usually within 24 hours of the order being placed.
- If a customer says they never received a tracking email, check the fulfillment dashboard
  directly — the order may still be in the "processing" queue, which can take up to 3 business
  days during high-volume periods (e.g. right after a referral promotion).

## Lost or delayed packages

1. Do not issue a replacement or refund until the estimated delivery window (see Timelines above)
   has fully elapsed.
2. Once elapsed, check the tracking link for a delivery scan. If it shows delivered but the
   customer says they didn't receive it, direct them to check with neighbors/building management
   first — this resolves the majority of "lost" cases within 48 hours.
3. If genuinely lost (no delivery scan after the window, or confirmed not received after step 2),
   offer a free reshipment. A cash refund for swag is only offered if the customer declines a
   reshipment.
4. International customs delays are common and are not considered "lost" until 30 days past the
   estimated window.
